# Egg Heist

Steal eggs from the guardians of 14 biomes, hatch them into pets, fuse them, and train on the treadmill to get faster.

**Play:** https://aysemusti.github.io/egg-heist/

## Playing with friends

1. Open the game and tap **Party**.
2. One player taps **Make party** and shares the party code.
3. Friends tap **Join party** and type the code.

Party mode connects players directly to each other through the free PeerJS service, so only make or join parties with people you know.

Progress is saved in your own browser.
